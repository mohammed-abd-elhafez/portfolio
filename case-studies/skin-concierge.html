Skin Concierge

Independent portfolio demo · Web design, UX writing, AI-assisted prototyping

Overview

Skin Concierge is an independent AI-assisted service-discovery experience built on Agentplace. It turns a public skincare menu into a guided customer journey with clear service cards, published-price comparisons and a copyable demo visit plan.

Design problem

Service names, durations, prices and contact information can be difficult for a first-time visitor to compare. The design goal was to reduce that friction without inventing missing information or implying medical suitability, live availability or clinic affiliation.

What I focused on

  • Clear information hierarchy for service names, prices, durations and source links.
  • Short conversational paths with explicit next steps instead of a generic FAQ wall.
  • Responsible UX copy that distinguishes published information from confirmed booking or medical advice.
  • A demo planning flow that lets a visitor revise and copy a preference note before contacting the business directly.

Review and boundaries

I reviewed the arrival screen, starting actions, price comparison, demo-plan creation and editing, copy feedback and official link destinations in Chrome. This is a personal portfolio demo, not a clinic-operated system. It does not access live availability, book appointments, collect payments or claim clinical suitability. Mobile-device testing remains limited.

Technology

Agentplace, structured content, UX writing, responsive interface review and AI-assisted prototyping.
